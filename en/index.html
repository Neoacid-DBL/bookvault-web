How it works

Three steps, then read.

No wizards, no sign-up. You bring the files and the app gets out of the way.

1
Step 1

Bring your books

Tap them in WhatsApp, your email or the file manager and open them with Balda, or pick files or a folder, which the app remembers. If you allow it, it also finds the ePub, MOBI, FB2 and CBZ books on your phone by itself. And the first time, two classics in your language are already waiting.

2
Step 2

Arrange them

Drag each book onto its shelf. Every shelf has a name and a theme — classic, modern, natural, fantasy, library or night — with its own wood and spine typeface.

3
Step 3

Read, or listen

Progress, highlights and stats save themselves, on the phone itself. And if you would rather listen, Balda reads the book aloud to you, even with the screen off.

Features

Everything you expect from a reader, plus the part most apps take away.

Owning books is also arranging them, marking them up, listening to them and knowing how much you have read.

Bookcase

Shelves you arrange

A real cabinet of shelves where you drag books with your finger. Each shelf gets a name and a theme, with its own wood and spine typeface.

Reader

Set up your way

Typeface, size, line height, margins and day, night, sepia and OLED themes. Turn pages with the volume buttons, and the screen stays on while you read.

Read aloud

It reads to you

It starts at the line in front of you, highlights the sentence being spoken and turns the page on its own. It keeps going with the screen off and answers to your headphones and the lock screen.

Search

Find any passage

Search inside the book without worrying about accents or capitals, and jump from one match to the next. In PDFs, on Android 15 and up.

Highlights

Highlight, note, bookmark

Highlight in five colours, leave notes and bookmarks, and export them whenever you like. Select text and the system menu offers the dictionary and translator.

Stats

It knows how much you read

Average speed, streaks, a consistency heatmap and weekly charts, all computed from your actual reading sessions on the device.

Goals

Goals you can see

A target per year or per period, telling you whether you are on track, cutting it close or at risk. And, if you want, a reminder that stays quiet on days you have already read.

Backup

Your library in one file

Save progress, highlights, shelves and, if you like, the books themselves to a .zip wherever you choose, and restore it on another phone. Balda uploads it nowhere.

Importing

Open it from wherever it is

A book in WhatsApp, your email or Downloads opens with Balda. Pick single files or a folder, or let Balda find the books on your phone by itself, if you give it permission.

Tablets

It stretches properly

On a wide screen the highlights panel opens beside the text instead of covering it, and the heatmap grows from 16 weeks to 52.

Offline

Airplane mode, same app

There is no server and no sync to fail. Keep the phone offline forever and the app behaves exactly the same.

Ads

Not one while you read

Ads live on the browsing screens. Inside a book there is no banner and no interruption mid-page, and no full-screen ad pops up during the first few days.

Formats

The ones already sitting in your downloads folder.

Nothing to convert and no desktop tool in the middle.

EPUBePub 2 and 3: highlights, search and read-aloud
MOBI·AZW3Kindle files without DRM, read like an ePub
FB2.fb2.zip too, with footnotes and images
PDFPinch to zoom; search on Android 15+
DOCX·ODTWord and LibreOffice documents, to read
TXT·MDPlain text, read-aloud too
CBZComics, page by page
CBRNo: nearly all are RAR5. Repack as CBZ

Kindle books bought on Amazon carry DRM and cannot be opened: Balda reads DRM-free MOBI and AZW3. CBR is left out on purpose: nearly all of them use RAR5 and no Android reader can open those, so the app tells you at import time.

Privacy

Your books never leave the phone.

No server, no account, no sync. Your library, your progress, your highlights and your stats live on the phone and are never sent anywhere. What leaves is the ads, the crash reports if you leave them on, and whatever you choose to share, when you send it.
