How to open FB2 files on Android

FB2 is very common in Russian and Eastern European ebook libraries, and increasingly elsewhere. If you downloaded one and your phone won't open it, here's how to read it without a computer.

Updated November 6, 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer

Install a reader that supports FB2, such as Balda (free, no account), tap the file — .fb2.zip too — and choose “Open with Balda”. It comes in with cover, table of contents and footnotes.

What an FB2 file is

FB2 (FictionBook) is an XML-based ebook format. Unlike EPUB, everything lives in a single text file: chapters, footnotes and even images, encoded inside. It's often zipped as .fb2.zip to save space.

Android has nothing built in to open it, and many popular readers don't accept it either.

Opening an FB2, step by step

  1. Install Balda from Google Play.
  2. Tap the .fb2 or .fb2.zip in your browser, chat or file manager and choose Balda. Or open Balda, tap “Add” and find it.
  3. The book comes in with its cover, title and author. Footnote markers take you to the note, and the table of contents lists the titled chapters.
💡

In Android's picker an FB2 may show up as a “BIN file”. Pick it anyway: Balda recognises it by name.

Balda converts the FB2 to EPUB on import and reads it like any EPUB from then on, with highlights, notes, search and read-aloud. If you turn on Settings → Books on your phone, it also finds the FB2 files in your storage by itself.

If the FB2 won't open or looks wrong

  • “The file is damaged”: the book's XML is broken, almost always from an incomplete download. Download it again.
  • Garbled letters instead of Cyrillic: Russian FB2 files are often encoded as windows-1251. Balda reads the encoding from the file's header; if it still looks wrong, the file declares the wrong encoding and it's worth finding another copy.
  • The contents just say “Chapter 1, Chapter 2…”: some FB2 files (for example, those exported by Calibre) don't title their chapters, so the contents number them. The book reads fine; only the contents are sparser.
  • Very large books: there is a 200 MB limit per file.

Frequently asked questions

FB2 or EPUB, which is better?

For reading, neither: both reflow to fit the screen. EPUB is more common in stores and libraries; FB2 in Russian-language collections. Balda reads both the same way.

Do I need to unzip the .fb2.zip?

No. Balda opens .fb2.zip directly.

Can I highlight and search in an FB2?

Yes. Because it comes in converted to EPUB, you get highlights in five colours, notes, bookmarks, search and read-aloud.

Do I need internet?

No. Once imported, the book reads offline and never leaves your phone.
