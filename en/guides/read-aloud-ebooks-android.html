How to have your phone read a book aloud

For cooking, driving or resting your eyes: your phone can read you a book that has no audiobook. All you need is the text-to-speech engine Android already has and a reader that uses it well.

Updated November 6, 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer

Open the book in Balda (free), tap the middle of the screen and press “Read aloud”. It starts at the line in front of you, keeps going with the screen off, and is controlled from the notification, the lock screen or your headphone buttons.

How read-aloud works

Android has a text-to-speech engine (usually Google's; on some phones, the manufacturer's too). Reading apps hand it the book's text sentence by sentence and the engine speaks it. Voice quality depends on that engine and the voices you have installed, not on the app.

Balda picks the voice from the book's language: an English book is read with an English voice even if your phone is set to Spanish. If the book doesn't state a language, it uses the phone's and tells you so.

Listening to a book, step by step

  1. Install Balda from Google Play and add your book (EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, DOCX, ODT or TXT).
  2. Open it, go to the page you want to start from and tap the middle of the screen.
  3. Press “Read aloud”. The sentence being spoken is highlighted and the page turns on its own.
  4. The bottom bar has speed, previous sentence, pause, next sentence and stop. The same controls appear in the notification and on the lock screen.
🎧

You can turn the screen off and keep listening. With Bluetooth headphones or car controls, pause, next and previous work just like with music; unplugging your headphones pauses the reading.

Improving or changing the voice

If the voice sounds robotic or isn't in the language you need, change it in Android's settings. The path depends on the manufacturer; it's usually Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech output or Settings → System → Languages → Text-to-speech output. From there you can choose the engine, install voices for other languages and try them out.

Some voices need a connection and others can be downloaded for offline use: it depends on your engine.

If you only have the book as a PDF, see whether an EPUB exists: besides being able to listen to it, it reads much better on a phone.

Frequently asked questions

Is it the same as an audiobook?

No: an audiobook is read by a person. Here the phone's synthetic voice reads, clearly but without performance. In exchange, it works with any book you have as text.

Can I change the speed?

Yes, from Balda's control bar, without stopping.

Does it drain the battery with the screen off?

Not much: with the screen off it costs about what playing audio does. Balda keeps the phone awake only while it's speaking.

Do the volume buttons turn pages while I listen?

No. While it's speaking, the volume buttons change the volume; when you're reading, they can turn pages if you have that turned on.
