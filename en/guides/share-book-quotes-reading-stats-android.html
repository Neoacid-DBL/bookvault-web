How to share book quotes and reading stats as images

A great line, a long streak or a finished book is nice to share. Here is how to turn them into good-looking images straight from your reader, and what stays private when you do.

Updated November 15, 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer

In Balda, open a highlight in the Annotations panel, or your streak, goal or year summary in the Progress and Goals tabs, and tap Share. You see the image first, then choose the app to send it to.

What you can share

  • A highlighted quote, from the Annotations panel.
  • Your streak, from the top of the Progress tab. If your current streak is zero, it shows your best one.
  • Your year in reading, from the Progress tab. It appears at the top, highlighted in pink, in December and January, and as a quieter row at the bottom the rest of the year.
  • Your yearly goal, from the goal ring in the Goals tab.
  • Books of the month, from the “This month” card in Goals.
  • A finished or rated book, from the book's detail sheet.

How to share a quote

  1. Highlight a passage in a book in Balda. See how to highlight and export notes.
  2. Open the Annotations panel and find the highlight.
  3. Tap Share. Balda shows the finished card, exactly as it will be sent.
  4. Tap Share again and pick an app from Android's share sheet: Instagram, WhatsApp, Messages or any other.

Long quotes are shortened to fit the card (about 360 characters).

Sharing stats and books

Cards are designed in a 4:5 shape, which fits the Instagram feed, and they are generated at a high resolution so they stay sharp. The colours are fixed, so the image looks the same on someone else's phone even if you read in dark mode. The text that goes with the image includes a link to Balda on Google Play.

For the cards that show what you read, namely books of the month and the year in reading, there is a switch “Show titles and covers”. Turn it off and the card shows plain spines without naming a favourite book.

What stays private

  • Nothing is shared unless you tap Share and choose where it goes. Balda has no connection to any social network.
  • You always see the card before it leaves the app.
  • Balda's usage statistics record only the type of card you shared, such as “quote” or “streak”. They never include the quote, the book title or your numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose the colours of the card?

The card colours are fixed. You choose what goes on it, such as whether to show titles and covers.

Do I need an Instagram account?

No. The image goes through Android's share sheet, so you can save it, send it in a chat or post it anywhere.

Can I share quotes from a PDF?

No, because highlights are not available in PDFs. They work in EPUB and the formats converted to EPUB.

Share the lines you love

Balda is free, needs no account, and only shares what you choose. For Android 8 and later.
