How to read MOBI and AZW3 books on Android

You have a folder of old Kindle books, or someone sent you a .mobi, and your phone doesn't know what to do with it. You don't need to convert it on a computer: you just need a reader that understands the format.

Updated November 6, 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer

Install a reader that opens MOBI and AZW3, such as Balda (free, no account), tap the file and choose “Open with Balda”. It works with DRM-free books; the ones you bought from Amazon's store carry DRM and only open in the Kindle app.

What MOBI and AZW3 are

They are Kindle's ebook formats. MOBI (also .azw and .prc) is the old one, inherited from Mobipocket. AZW3, also called KF8, is the modern one: inside it is almost an EPUB — HTML and CSS — packaged differently, so it keeps chapters, table of contents, images and the book's typeface.

Many modern .mobi files are actually “combined”: they hold an old version and a KF8 one. A good reader uses the modern one, which looks better.

Opening a MOBI or AZW3, step by step

  1. Install Balda from Google Play. No sign-up.
  2. If the book came by WhatsApp, email or Drive, tap it and choose Balda under “Open with”. If it's already on the phone, open Balda and tap “Add”.
  3. The book lands in your library with its cover, title, author and table of contents, and opens at the first page.
💡

In Android's file picker, .mobi files sometimes show up as a “BIN file” or “PRC file”. That's normal: Android doesn't know the file type, but Balda recognises them by name and opens them anyway.

If you have lots of them around the phone, Settings → Books on your phone lets Balda find them on its own: it picks up the MOBI, AZW3, EPUB, FB2 and CBZ files in storage and adds new ones each time you open it. It's optional and needs the “All files access” permission.

What you keep when reading it

Balda converts the MOBI or AZW3 to EPUB when you import it, on the phone itself, and from then on reads it like any EPUB. In practice you get:

  • Cover, table of contents with parts and chapters, illustrations and the book's styling.
  • Highlights in five colours, notes and bookmarks.
  • Search inside the book and read-aloud.
  • Typeface, size, margins and paper (day, sepia, night or OLED) the way you like.

One detail: the book's details will say “EPUB”, because that's what the app stores. Your original file is never touched.

Books bought on Amazon: DRM

Books you buy from the Kindle store are protected with DRM: they are encrypted so that only the Kindle app with your account can open them. No independent reader can open them, Balda included. If you try, it tells you the book has DRM instead of showing a blank page.

DRM-free MOBI and AZW3 files do open: those from publishers and authors who don't use DRM, from Project Gutenberg and other public-domain libraries, or ones you make yourself with Calibre from an EPUB.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to convert the MOBI to EPUB with Calibre?

No. Balda does that conversion by itself when importing, on the phone. Calibre is still handy for managing a big library on a computer, but you don't need it to read.

Does it open Kindle KFX files?

No. Balda opens DRM-free .mobi, .azw, .azw3 and .prc files. The KFX format used by recent Kindle apps isn't one of them.

Are my books uploaded anywhere?

No. Balda has no account and no server: conversion and reading happen on the phone, and your books never leave it.

Is it free?

Yes. There are ads on the library and other browsing screens, never while you read.
