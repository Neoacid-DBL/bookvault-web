How to track your reading on Android

A goal and a streak are a simple way to read more. Here is how to set them up, what the numbers mean and how they are counted, without sending your reading anywhere.

Updated November 15, 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer

In Balda, open the Goals tab and tap Set a goal: choose books per year, books per month, pages per day or minutes per day. The Progress tab shows your streak, speed and reading activity. Everything is calculated on your phone.

What the Progress tab shows

  • Current streak and your longest streak, counted in days.
  • Pages and reading time for today, this week and all time.
  • Pages read in the last 30 days and your reading speed over the last 12 weeks.
  • A reading activity heatmap: 16 weeks on a phone, 52 weeks on a tablet or foldable.
  • Your book ratings and, in December and January, a summary of your year in reading.

While you read, Balda also estimates how much time you have left in the chapter and in the book. The estimate gets sharper the more you read.

Setting a reading goal

  1. Install Balda from Google Play and open the Goals tab.
  2. Tap Set a goal and choose the type: Books per year, Books per month, Pages per day or Minutes per day.
  3. Pick how much, then tap Create goal.

For book goals, Balda tells you whether you are on track, slightly behind or at risk of missing it, based on the days left. Daily goals are measured as an average over calendar days, so reading a lot on one day does not cover a whole month of daily targets.

How reading is counted

The numbers only mean something if they are honest, so Balda is strict about what counts as reading:

  • Only pages you turn forward in sequence count. Jumping with the table of contents, the slider, search or an annotation is navigating, not reading.
  • Reading speed is capped at a realistic maximum for text books, so skimming through pages quickly will not inflate your stats.
  • Sessions shorter than 30 seconds, or without any pages read, are ignored.
  • Listening to a book read aloud with the screen off is not counted, because the app is not turning pages.
  • A finished book stays finished: reopening it does not reset its finish date or move it into a new year.

Widgets and reminders

  • Home screen widgets show your goal progress and your reading for today, so you can see them without opening the app.
  • Daily reminder (optional, off by default): after you have read on a couple of different days, Balda offers a reminder at a time you choose. You can also turn it on in Settings → Daily reminder. It only goes off on days you have not read yet, and Android asks for notification permission when you turn it on. After a few unanswered reminders, it slows down to once a week.

Sharing your progress

Your streak, yearly goal, books of the month and year in reading can be turned into an image for Instagram, WhatsApp or anywhere else. You see the image before it is sent, and you can hide titles and covers. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share.

Frequently asked questions

Is my reading data uploaded anywhere?

No. Progress, sessions and stats are stored on your phone. The only data that leaves the device is what you choose to share.

Will I lose my streak if I change phones?

Your stats are part of the backup in Settings → Backup, so restoring it on the new phone brings them back. See how to back up your library.

Do comics and PDFs count?

Reading sessions are recorded whichever format you read.
