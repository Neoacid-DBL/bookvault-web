How to add many ebooks to your Android reader at once

Importing a whole collection one book at a time would take forever. Here are the three ways to bring in many books at once, and what to expect when you do.

Updated November 15, 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer

In Balda, tap Add and select as many files as you like at once. To bring in a whole collection, add its folder in Settings → Book folders, or let Balda find the books on your phone with Books on your phone.

Picking many files

  1. Install Balda from Google Play and tap Add in the library.
  2. In the dialog, choose Choose books. Select several files in Android's picker (press and hold one, then tap the others).
  3. Balda imports them one by one and shows its progress: “Importing 3 of 20 books…”

Adding a folder

If your books live in one folder, give Balda access to it: choose Add folder in the same dialog, or go to Settings → Book folders → Add folder. Balda adds what is inside and checks the folder again each time you open the app, so new books you drop in later appear by themselves. You can also tap Check now to look right away.

Android does not allow you to grant the whole Downloads folder, but a folder inside it works. Create something like Download/Books and use that.

Letting Balda find your books

If you turn on Books on your phone, Balda searches the whole phone for EPUB, FB2, AZW3, MOBI and CBZ files, including those in Downloads and WhatsApp, and adds new ones each time you open it. Android asks you to allow “All files access”, which you can turn off whenever you like. PDFs and text files are never added this way, because they could be anything. To bring in PDFs in bulk, put them in a folder and add that folder.

Reading the import summary

When a batch finishes, Balda shows a short message such as “5 books added · 2 were already there · 3 couldn't be imported”. Tap View to see the name of each book that failed and the reason. Typical reasons:

  • “The file is over the 200 MB limit”: too large to import.
  • “The book could not be read: the file is damaged”: an incomplete download or a corrupt file. Download it again.
  • “This Kindle book is DRM-protected”: only DRM-free AZW3 and MOBI can be read.
  • “CBR comics cannot be opened”: convert to CBZ first. See how to convert CBR to CBZ.

Duplicates and deletions

  • The same book twice. If a file is identical to one already in your library, Balda recognises it and does not add it again.
  • Different books with the same file name. Neither overwrites the other. The second gets a number, such as “name (2)”.
  • Balda copies each book into its own storage, so a collection takes up space twice. Your original files are never changed or deleted.
  • A book you delete in Balda stays deleted. It will not be re-added by a folder check unless the original file changes. Deleting removes the copied file and its reading progress.

Frequently asked questions

How many books can I add?

There is no fixed limit on the library. Each file can be up to 200 MB, and each bookcase shelf holds up to 60 books, but the library itself can hold as many as your storage allows.

Do I have to keep the original files?

No. Once a book is imported, Balda has its own copy. Keeping the originals is still a good idea as a backup.

Will it organise my books?

New books arrive unshelved. Drag them onto shelves in the Shelf tab. See how to organise your library with shelves.
