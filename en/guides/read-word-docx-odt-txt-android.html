How to read Word, ODT and TXT files as books on Android

A long manuscript, a fan story or a pile of class notes is far easier to read in an ebook reader than in a word processor. Here is how to open them, and what to expect.

Updated November 15, 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer

Open the .docx, .odt or .txt file with a reader that supports them, such as Balda. Word and OpenDocument files are converted to an ebook when you import them, so you get adjustable text, night mode, highlights and read aloud.

Why read documents like a book

Word processors are built for writing, not reading on a phone. An ebook reader lets you pick the font, size, line spacing and margins, switch to a dark or sepia page, resume exactly where you stopped and, if you want, have the text read aloud.

Opening a document, step by step

  1. Install Balda from Google Play.
  2. Tap the document in your file manager, email or chat and choose Balda under “Open with”. Or open Balda, tap “Add” and select it.
  3. Balda converts it to an ebook on import and adds it to your library. From then on it reads like any other book.
💡

Writing in Google Docs? Use File → Download and choose Microsoft Word (.docx), OpenDocument Format (.odt) or EPUB Publication (.epub), then open the file on your phone.

Unlike EPUB, FB2 and the other book formats, DOCX, ODT and PDF files are not picked up by the automatic search for books on your phone, because a document on a phone can be an invoice or anything else. You add them yourself, with “Open with”, or from a folder you have granted in Settings → Book folders.

What carries over, and what does not

Balda reads these documents; it does not edit them.

  • Carries over: text, paragraph styles, bold, italics and underline, headings (which become chapters and the table of contents), images, tables, lists, links and footnotes or endnotes.
  • Does not carry over: multi-column layouts, floating text boxes, headers and footers, comments and tracked changes. Old .doc files are not recognised, so save them as .docx first.
  • Colours and fonts from the document are replaced by your own reader settings, so the text matches the rest of your library.
  • Password-protected documents appear as damaged files.

Because the converted document is an ebook, you can highlight, add notes and bookmarks, search and use read aloud on it, just as with an EPUB.

Plain text and Markdown

.txt files open directly, in paged or scrolling mode. .md files are read as plain text: the Markdown symbols (#, ##) stay visible, because there is no Markdown renderer. Read aloud works on text files.

Documents of up to 200 MB can be imported.

Frequently asked questions

Can I edit the document in Balda?

No. Balda is a reader. Edit the original in your word processor and import it again.

Will it keep my chapters?

Yes, if they are real headings. Balda builds the table of contents from heading styles, or from the document's own table of contents when that has more entries.

What about PDF?

PDF opens in its own viewer, with pinch to zoom. Text search in PDFs needs Android 15 or later.
