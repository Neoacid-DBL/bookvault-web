A free ebook reader with no ads while you read

Free apps are paid for somehow. If you have to choose between an ad-supported reader and a paid one, it is worth knowing exactly how the ads behave. Here is the full picture for Balda.

Updated November 15, 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer

Balda is free and supported by ads, but you never see an ad while you read: no banner in the reader and no breaks mid-page. Ads appear on browsing screens, and a full-screen ad may show when you close a book after a real reading session.

Where there are no ads

  • In the reader. No banner, no pop-up and no interruption while a book, PDF or comic is open.
  • During your first days. No full-screen ad appears in the first days after you install the app.
  • After a quick look. Closing a book you barely opened does not trigger a full-screen ad. It only happens after a real reading session.

Where ads do appear

  • Banners sit at the bottom of browsing screens such as the Library, the Shelf, Progress and Goals. They do not appear until an ad has actually loaded, so there is no empty gap.
  • A full-screen ad may show when you close a book after reading for a while. There is a pause of several minutes between them and a small limit per session. Sometimes closing a book asks for a Play Store review instead, and in that case there is no ad.
  • An optional video unlocks the extra bookshelf themes for 24 hours. It only plays if you tap to unlock them. See organizing your library with shelves.

There is no paid version at the moment, so there is no way to remove ads by paying.

Consent and your ad choices

In Europe, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Balda asks for your consent before the first ad request, with an option for non-personalised ads. You can reopen that choice at any time in Settings → Ads → Ad privacy options. In other regions the form is not required and the entry does not appear.

Ads use Android's advertising ID, as in any app with ads. You can also manage it in your phone's own privacy settings.

Offline and privacy

The only things in Balda that use the internet are the ads and, if you leave it on, crash reporting (Settings → Diagnostics → Send crash reports). Your books, reading progress, highlights and stats stay on your phone. If you read offline, you will not see ads, since there is no connection to load them. See the reader with no account and no cloud for the full picture.

Frequently asked questions

Will ads ever appear mid-chapter?

No. The reader is kept clear of ads, and a full-screen ad can only appear when you leave a book.

Can I pay to remove ads?

Not right now. The app is free and ad-supported.

Why do you use ads at all?

They let the app stay free with no account and no subscription, while keeping ads out of the one place where they would be most annoying: while you are reading.
