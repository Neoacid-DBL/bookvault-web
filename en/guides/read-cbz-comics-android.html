How to read CBZ comics on Android

A CBZ file is a comic packed into a single file. Android has nothing built in to read it, but opening one takes a few taps once you have a reader that supports the format.

Updated November 15, 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer

Install a reader that supports CBZ, such as Balda (free, no account), tap the .cbz file and choose “Open with Balda”. The comic opens one page per screen and Balda remembers where you left off.

What a CBZ file is

CBZ (Comic Book ZIP) is simply a ZIP archive of page images, usually JPG or PNG, renamed to .cbz. CBR is the same idea using RAR compression instead. Both are the standard way digital comic collections are shared.

Because a CBZ is only images, it opens the same everywhere and does not depend on fonts or layout the way an ebook does.

Opening a CBZ comic

  1. Install Balda from Google Play.
  2. Tap the .cbz file in your browser, chat app or file manager and choose Balda. Or open Balda, tap “Add” and select one or several comics at once.
  3. The comic lands on your shelf with its cover. Tap it to start reading.

Letting Balda find your comics

If you keep a lot of comics, there are two ways to avoid adding them one by one:

  • Book folders. In Settings → Book folders, tap “Add folder” and pick the folder where your comics live. Balda checks it every time you open the app and adds the new ones. Android does not allow granting the whole Downloads folder, but a folder inside it, such as Downloads/Comics, works.
  • Books on your phone. If you turn this on, Balda looks through the whole phone for EPUB, FB2, AZW3, MOBI and CBZ files, including Downloads and WhatsApp. It needs Android's “All files access” permission, which you can revoke at any time.

Balda copies each comic into its own storage, so it takes up space twice. Your original files are never touched.

Reading the comic

  • Each page fills the screen, one page at a time, on a black background. Swipe sideways to turn the page, or use the volume keys.
  • Tap the centre of the screen to show or hide the controls.
  • Balda remembers the page you were on in every comic.
  • Comics sit on your shelves next to your books, so “Reading now” can hold both.
💡

The comic reader is simple by design: pages are fitted to the screen and there is no zoom. It suits phones and tablets best for comics whose lettering is readable at that size. Read aloud does not work on comics.

If a comic will not open

  • It is a CBR file. Balda does not import CBR, because most of them use a newer RAR format that Android readers cannot open. Convert it to CBZ first, which takes a minute: see how to convert CBR to CBZ.
  • “The comic pages could not be read.” The archive is probably damaged or incomplete. Download it again.
  • It will not import and says the file is too large. There is a 200 MB limit per file.
  • The file shows up as “BIN” or an unknown type in Android's picker. Pick it anyway: Balda recognises it by its name.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between CBZ and CBR?

Both are archives of page images. CBZ uses ZIP, which every device can open. CBR uses RAR, which is far less widely supported on Android. If you have a choice, CBZ is the safer format.

Can I read comics and books in the same app?

Yes. Balda handles EPUB, FB2, MOBI, AZW3, PDF, TXT, DOCX, ODT and CBZ together, and you can put them all on the same shelves.

Do I need internet?

No. Once a comic is imported it reads offline and never leaves your phone.
