How to read PDFs comfortably on Android

PDFs were made for paper, and a phone screen is not paper. A few habits make long PDFs much easier to read, and it helps to know what a PDF viewer can and cannot do.

Updated November 15, 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer

Open the PDF in Balda, scroll down the pages, and pinch to zoom in up to 4×. On Android 15 or later, use Search in PDF to find text. Balda remembers the page you were on. If the same book exists as an EPUB, that will usually read better on a phone.

Opening a PDF

  1. Install Balda from Google Play.
  2. Tap the PDF in your browser, chat app, email or file manager and choose Balda under “Open with”. Or open Balda, tap “Add” and select it.
  3. Balda adds it to your library and opens it. Pages are drawn to the width of the screen and you scroll down through them.
💡

Balda's automatic search for books on your phone does not add PDFs, because a PDF on a phone can be an invoice or any other document. Add the ones you want yourself, or from a folder you grant in Settings → Book folders.

Zooming and moving around

  • Pinch to zoom up to 4×, which is useful for small print, tables and diagrams. When you let go, the pages are redrawn sharply at the new size.
  • Volume keys turn a page at a time if you turn on “Turn pages with volume keys” in the reading settings: down goes forward, up goes back.
  • Tap the centre of the screen to show or hide the controls.
  • Balda remembers where you stopped and opens the book on the same page next time.

Searching the text

On phones with Android 15 or later, the reader has a Search in PDF button. Type a word and Balda counts the results, highlights them on the page and lets you jump to the previous or next one. Earlier versions of Android do not provide the feature Balda relies on, so on those phones the button does not appear.

Search only finds real text. A scanned book is a stack of photographs of pages, so there is no text to search.

What a PDF viewer cannot do

  • No reflow. The page stays the size it was designed for. You zoom instead of changing the font size.
  • No highlights, notes or bookmarks. Those work in EPUB and the formats converted to EPUB, not in PDF.
  • No read aloud for PDFs.
  • Size limit. Files over 200 MB are not imported.

If you see “The PDF could not be opened”, the file may be damaged or incomplete. Download it again.

When to look for an EPUB instead

For a novel or any text-heavy book, an EPUB is almost always more comfortable than the PDF: the text reflows to your screen, you can change the font and use night mode, and highlights and read aloud work. Check whether the book is available as an EPUB, or read our comparison of ebook formats. Keep the PDF for books where the layout matters, such as textbooks, manuals and anything full of diagrams.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the text so small?

Most PDFs are laid out for A4 or Letter paper. On a phone you see the whole page at a reduced size, so pinch to zoom.

Can I convert a PDF to a reflowable book?

Tools like Calibre can try, but results vary a lot with how the PDF was made, and scanned books do not convert well. See how to move books from Calibre to Android.

Do I need internet?

No. Once the PDF is in your library, it reads offline.
