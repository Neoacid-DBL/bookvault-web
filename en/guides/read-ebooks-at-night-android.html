How to read ebooks at night on Android

A white page is hard to love at midnight. Here is how to set up your reader for the dark, and what else you can adjust to make long reading sessions easier.

Updated November 15, 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer

In Balda, tap the centre of the page to show the controls, open Reading settings and choose the Night or OLED page colour. Then adjust text size, line spacing and margins until it feels right. The setting is remembered for next time.

The four page colours

  • Day: the classic light page, best in daylight.
  • Sepia: a warm, paper-like tone that is softer than pure white.
  • Night: light text on a dark page.
  • OLED: light text on a true black page. On phones with an OLED screen, black pixels are switched off, so this is the darkest option and can save a little battery.

These are page colours for reading. They are separate from the look of the app itself, which you set in Settings → Appearance (follow the system, always light or always dark).

Setting it up, step by step

  1. Install Balda from Google Play and open a book.
  2. Tap the centre of the screen to show the controls, then open Reading settings.
  3. Under theme, pick Night or OLED. The page changes straight away, so you can try each one.

Text settings that help

  • Text size: a slightly larger size lets you hold the phone further away and relax.
  • Line spacing: more air between lines can make long paragraphs easier to follow.
  • Margins: wider side margins give shorter lines.
  • Font: choose Book (the font the publisher chose), Serif, Sans or Dyslexia, which adds extra spacing between letters and words in EPUB books.
  • Continuous scroll: if you prefer scrolling to turning pages, switch it on in the same menu.
  • Volume keys: “Turn pages with volume keys” lets you read without touching the screen: down goes forward, up goes back.

Screen and sound tips

  • Keep the screen from sleeping. Balda keeps the screen on while you read and lets it sleep after 10 minutes without touching it or turning a page, so it does not drain the battery if you fall asleep.
  • Lower the brightness. Your phone's own tools help here, such as the brightness slider, Extra dim or a night-light or eye-comfort mode where your phone has one.
  • Close your eyes and listen. Read aloud keeps going with the screen off, and you can pause or skip sentences from the lock screen or your headphones. It works with EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, DOCX, ODT and TXT books, but not with PDFs or comics.

Frequently asked questions

Does the dark theme work on every book?

It applies when you read text books, such as EPUB and the formats converted to EPUB. Comics always display on a black background.

Is OLED better than Night?

It depends on your screen. On an OLED display, true black is darker and uses less power. On an LCD screen, Night and OLED look much the same.

Will my settings be remembered?

Yes. They apply to all your books, and they are included in a backup.
