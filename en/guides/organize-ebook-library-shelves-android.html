How to organize your ebook library on Android with shelves

A long list of titles is hard to browse. A bookcase with shelves you name yourself is much closer to how you organise real books. Here is how it works.

Updated November 15, 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer

In Balda, open the Shelf tab. Your books wait in Unshelved; press and hold one and drag it onto a shelf. Tap New shelf to add more, and rename or restyle each one from Shelf options.

Getting started with the bookcase

Balda comes with three shelves: Reading now, To read and Finished. Any book you have not placed yet sits in the Unshelved tray at the bottom.

  1. Install Balda from Google Play, add some books and open the Shelf tab.
  2. Press and hold a book in the Unshelved tray and drag it onto a shelf. The shelf you are about to drop it on is highlighted.
  3. You can move a book from one shelf to another the same way, and if you use TalkBack, each book also offers a Move to another shelf action, so dragging is not the only way.

The first book on each shelf stands facing out, with its cover showing, and the rest sit as spines whose width reflects how long the book is. Comics can sit on the same shelves as your books.

Creating and managing shelves

  • Add a shelf: tap New shelf.
  • Rename, restyle or reorder: open Shelf options to change the shelf name and theme, or to move it up or down.
  • Fill a shelf quickly: Fill from “Unshelved” puts your loose books on the shelf in one go.
  • Delete a shelf: the books are not deleted. They go back to Unshelved.

A book lives on one shelf at a time, and each shelf holds up to 60 books. If you see “This shelf is full”, start another one.

Shelf themes

Each shelf has its own theme, with its own wood and spine lettering. Classic, Modern and Natural are available from day one. Fantasy, Library and Nocturne unlock for 24 hours when you choose to watch a video, and only if you ask for it.

Finding books in a big library

The shelves are for browsing. In the Library tab, you can search by title and filter by Reading, To read, Finished or Rating, so you can always get to a specific book quickly.

Ways to organise

  • By status: keep the default Reading now, To read and Finished.
  • By genre or topic: fiction, history, work, language learning.
  • By series or author: one shelf per series you are working through.
  • By format: a separate shelf for comics.
  • By mood: “Weekend”, “Commute”, “Bedtime”.

Frequently asked questions

Do shelves sync to my other devices?

No. Shelves live on your phone and are included in the backup you can save in Settings → Backup. See how to back up your library.

Does deleting a shelf delete my books?

No. The books go back to the Unshelved tray and stay in your library.

Can I use the bookcase with a tablet?

Yes. On wide screens the navigation moves to the side and the layout adapts.
