How to highlight and export your notes from ebooks on Android

Highlights are only useful if you can find them again. Here is how to mark up an ebook on your phone, gather everything in one place and take your notes with you.

Updated November 15, 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer

In Balda, press and hold a word and drag to select text, then tap a colour to highlight it or Add note. Open the Annotations panel to review everything, and use its menu to export your notes as Markdown (.md) or plain text (.txt).

Highlighting and adding notes

  1. Open a book in Balda.
  2. Press and hold a word, then drag the handles to select the passage.
  3. A small toolbar appears. Tap one of the five colours to highlight it, or choose a custom colour. Tap Add note to attach your own comment to the passage.
💡

The menu Android shows when you select text also offers Copy, Translate and any dictionary apps you have installed. Balda has no dictionary of its own: the selected text only goes to the app you choose, when you tap it.

Bookmarks

To mark a place without highlighting anything, use Bookmark page from the reader controls. Balda also remembers where you stopped in every book, so you only need bookmarks for places you want to come back to.

Reviewing your annotations

Open the Annotations panel from the reader. It lists every highlight, note and bookmark in the book, and you can filter the list by type. On a tablet or a foldable, the panel opens beside the text instead of covering it.

Exporting your notes

  1. In the Annotations panel, open the menu and choose Export annotations.
  2. Pick Export as Markdown (.md) or Export as text (.txt).
  3. Use Android's share sheet to save the file or send it to another app, such as a notes app, email or cloud storage.

The file includes the book's title and author, the export date, and each highlight, note and bookmark with its page number. It is a plain text file, so it opens in any editor and is easy to paste into your own notes.

You can also turn a single highlight into a quote image to share with friends. Balda shows you the card before it goes anywhere, and nothing is shared unless you tap Share.

Which formats support annotations

  • Yes: EPUB, and the formats Balda converts to EPUB when you import them: FB2, MOBI, AZW3 (without DRM), DOCX and ODT.
  • No: PDF, plain text (TXT and MD) and CBZ comics. You can read these in Balda, but you cannot highlight them.

Your annotations are stored on your phone and are part of the backup you can save in Settings → Backup. See how to back up your library.

Frequently asked questions

Can I export the notes of all my books at once?

Export works one book at a time, from that book's Annotations panel.

Do my highlights sync to other devices?

No. There is no cloud sync, so highlights stay on the phone where you made them. To move them, restore a backup on the other device or export them as a file.

Can I highlight a PDF?

No. Highlights, notes and bookmarks work in EPUB and the formats converted to EPUB, but not in PDF, plain text or comics.

Mark up your books
