How to back up your ebook library and move it to a new phone

A reader with no account and no cloud means your library lives on your phone. That is great for privacy, and it makes a backup worth five minutes. Here is how to do it and how to restore it.

Updated November 15, 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer

In Balda, go to Settings → Backup → Save a backup, choose whether to include the books, and save the .zip file wherever you like. On the new phone, install Balda and use Restore a backup to load it.

Why back up

Without an account or a sync service, nothing stores your library but your phone. If the phone is lost, broken or reset, your reading progress, highlights and shelves go with it. A backup is a single file that you keep wherever you trust, and Balda never uploads it anywhere.

Saving a backup

  1. Open Balda and go to Settings, then Backup.
  2. Tap Save a backup. Balda asks “Include the books?”
  3. Choose Include books to be able to restore on another phone without importing the books again, at the cost of a larger file. Choose Data only for a small file with your library data, progress, annotations, shelves and settings.
  4. Pick where to save the .zip with Android's file picker: your phone's storage, a USB drive or a cloud-drive folder. When you see “Backup saved”, you are done.
💡

Copy the .zip off the phone, to a computer or a cloud drive. A backup that only lives on the phone does not help if the phone is lost.

Restoring on a new phone

  1. Copy the .zip file to the new phone.
  2. Install Balda, then open Settings → Backup → Restore a backup and pick the file.
  3. Confirm. Balda warns you that your current library will be replaced by the one in the backup, then restarts with everything back: books, progress, annotations, shelves and settings.

Balda checks the file before touching anything, so a damaged or wrong file is rejected and leaves your current library as it was.

What is included

  • Always: your library list, reading progress, highlights, notes and bookmarks, shelves, goals, reading stats and settings.
  • Only if you choose “Include books”: the book files themselves.
  • Not carried over: permissions that Android grants to each phone separately. If you used Settings → Book folders, add those folders again on the new phone.

If you saved a data-only backup, the books will not come with it. Import the same book files on the new phone and they take their place in your library with the progress and highlights you saved.

If something goes wrong

  • “That file isn't a Balda backup”: the file is not a backup made by Balda, or it is damaged. Check you picked the right .zip.
  • “This backup comes from a newer version of Balda”: update Balda from Google Play and try again.
  • “The backup could not be saved”: check that the location has free space, or choose a different folder.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I back up?

Whenever you would hate to lose what you have done since the last one: after finishing a book, after a long run of highlights, or before changing phones or resetting this one.

Can I use the backup to sync two devices?

Not as a sync. Restoring replaces the library on the device, so it works for moving or recovering, not for keeping two phones in step.

Does the backup contain my account or passwords?

There is no account in Balda, so there is nothing of that kind to back up.

Keep your library safe

Balda is free, needs no account, and your backups stay wherever you put them. For Android 8 and later.
