Where are my downloaded ebooks on Android?

You downloaded a book and now it seems to have vanished. It did not: Android just does not show you where it went. Here is how to find it and open it.

Updated November 15, 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer

Open your phone’s Files app and look in Downloads. That is where browsers save books by default. Tap the file and choose a reader such as Balda. Books from WhatsApp, email or other apps may be somewhere else, and a reader that scans for books can find them for you.

Where Android keeps your downloads

  • From a browser: in the Downloads folder. Chrome also lists everything in its own Downloads page (⋮ menu → Downloads).
  • From WhatsApp: documents you receive are saved in WhatsApp's own folders, which many file managers hide. The quickest way to open one is to tap it in the chat and choose a reader.
  • From email: open the message, tap the attachment and choose a reader, or save it to Downloads from the attachment menu.
  • From cloud storage: open the file in the drive app and use Open with, or download it to the phone first.

Finding a book by name or type

Your file manager (Files by Google, My Files on Samsung phones and similar) usually has a search box and a “Recent” tab. Search for part of the title, or for the extension: .epub, .pdf, .mobi, .azw3, .fb2 or .cbz. Sorting by date puts the newest download first.

Opening the book

  1. Install a reader that supports your format. Balda is free, needs no account and opens EPUB, FB2, MOBI, AZW3, PDF, TXT, DOCX, ODT and CBZ.
  2. Tap the file in your file manager, browser or chat and choose Balda under “Open with”. If Android asks whether to open it just once or always, “Just once” is fine.
  3. The book is added to your library and opens where you tapped.
💡

In Android's file picker, FB2, MOBI and some other ebook files can appear as a “BIN file” or “PRC file”. Pick them anyway: Balda recognises the format by the file name.

Letting your reader find them

If books pile up across Downloads and chat apps, Balda can collect them for you. Turn on Books on your phone (Android will ask for “All files access”, which you can turn off later). Each time you open Balda it looks for EPUB, FB2, AZW3, MOBI and CBZ files on the phone, including Downloads and WhatsApp, and adds the new ones. It never adds PDFs or text files this way, because those could be anything.

Prefer to stay in control? In Settings → Book folders you can pick specific folders instead. Android does not allow the whole Downloads folder, but a subfolder like Downloads/Books works.

If it still looks wrong

  • The file is called .acsm: that is not the book but a licence ticket for a DRM-protected one. See how to open EPUB files on Android.
  • The file is a .zip: some sites package the book in a ZIP. Extract it first. The exception is .fb2.zip, which Balda opens directly.
  • The download stopped halfway: a very small file or an “incomplete” error usually means the download did not finish. Download it again.
  • The format is not supported: Balda does not open CBR or DRM-protected books. See converting CBR to CBZ.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I see the WhatsApp folder?

Recent versions of Android can restrict apps and some file managers from showing other apps’ folders. Opening the document straight from the chat always works.

Will Balda delete my original file?

No. Balda copies the book into its own storage and never touches the original.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once a book is in Balda it reads without internet.
